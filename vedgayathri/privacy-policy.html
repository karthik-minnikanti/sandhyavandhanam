Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10/03/2026

Thank you for using our application. This Privacy Policy describes how the VedGayatri application handles information. We do not operate servers that collect personal data from the app; optional settings you choose are kept on your device only, as described below.

Information collection and use

We do not collect, transmit to ourselves, or process personal identifiers (such as your name, email, or phone number) through the app. We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking technologies. We do not use cookies or similar tracking in the app for profiling you across apps or websites.

The app may store optional preferences only on your device using local device storage (for example: text size, whether you have completed introductory hints, your last-read section for convenience, an optional activity streak for your personal use, and reminder times if you enable reminders). This information stays on your phone or tablet and is not sent to us or to third-party services by the app.

We do not collect information such as:

  • Personal identifiers for transmission to us (name, email address, phone number)
  • Location information
  • Device identifiers for analytics or advertising
  • Usage analytics sent to the developer or advertisers

Permissions

If you choose to enable optional Sandhyā reminders, the app may ask for notification permission so your device can show scheduled local reminders. You can decline; the rest of the app works without notifications. The app does not require access to the camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or your precise location for its features.

Third-party services

This application does not integrate third-party services that collect your personal data for analytics or advertising. Your device operating system (Apple, Google) may process notifications or storage according to its own policies, which are outside this app’s control.

Data security

Because we do not receive or host your data on our servers, there is no account database maintained by us for this app. Information stored locally on your device is subject to your device’s security (passcode, encryption settings, etc.).

Children’s privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 for our own use. Because we do not operate data collection from the app as described above, the app may be used by people of all ages with appropriate supervision.

Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
